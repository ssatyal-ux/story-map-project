Philadelphia’s Housing Need

This story map examines the geographic distribution of affordable housing in Philadelphia and how it relates to neighborhood housing conditions and socioeconomic characteristics.

Data Domain

The project will use data on affordable housing production, Philadelphia Housing Authority properties, property assessments, household income, rent, and housing-cost burden.

These datasets will be used to compare the existing supply of affordable housing with neighborhood-level indicators of housing need and potential investment opportunities.

Target Audience and Persona

Shubhanga, an Affordable Housing Appraiser

Shubhanga is a real estate appraiser who specializes in affordable multifamily housing. He needs reliable geographic and socioeconomic information to evaluate neighborhood conditions, understand local housing demand, and identify areas that may support future affordable housing investment.

This story map is designed for appraisers, valuation professionals, affordable housing developers, and other practitioners evaluating future investment opportunities in Philadelphia.
