About the Story Map:

This story map is a surface-level look at where affordable housing gets built in Philadelphia, and why that matters for site selection advocacy. Using open city data, it compares the city's Market Value Analysis with thirty years of subsidized housing production. It shows that most affordable units end up in the weakest housing markets and very few go to the strongest. It then zooms into one strong-market neighborhood and points to vacant land that could still be used. The goal is to start a conversation, not to settle one: it doesn't account for land costs, zoning, or current parcel ownership.
